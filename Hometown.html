KARNAL

Known as the "Rice Bowl of India" and the birthplace of astronaut Kalpana Chawla, Karnal is a vibrant Haryana city blending rich agricultural research heritage with legendary Mahabharata history.

The National Pride of Karnal

Dr.Kalpana Chawla

Born in Karnal, Haryana, Kalpana Chawla was an aeronautical engineer who made history as the first Indian-born woman to fly into space. She flew her first mission in 1997 aboard Space Shuttle Columbia (STS-87) as a primary robotic arm operator.

National Dairy Research Institute

The ICAR-NDRI in Karnal is India's premier institution for dairy education, research, and technology.It plays a crucial role in advancing cattle breeding, cloning, and milk processing to boost the nation's agricultural economy.

Central Soil Salinity Research Institute

Established in 1969 under ICAR-CSSRI in Karnal is India's premier research organization dedicated to reclaiming salt-affected agricultural land. It develops innovative technologies for managing soil salinity, subsurface drainage, and the productive use of poor-quality groundwater.

Top Spots in Karnal

Karnal Haveli

Karnal Haveli is a popular Punjabi-themed resort and restaurant known for its traditional decor, village-style ambiance, and rich North Indian cuisine.

Karna Lake

Karna Lake is a picturesque man-made lake and popular tourist complex situated along NH-44.

Cantonment Church Tower

Built in 1806, the 35-meter-tall Cantonment Church Tower is a historic four-story British colonial landmark in Karnal that originally belonged to St. James' Church.

A perfect blend of sacred Mahabharata history, pioneering scientific achievements, and warm Haryanvi hospitality, Karnal truly stands as a proud emblem of India's past, present, and future.
